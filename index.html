Where AI comes in

Rules catch the obvious. AI catches the awkward.

Fast, free rules check every post first. Then, only for the posts that got flagged, an AI second opinion reads the post next to the headlines, explains the risk in one line, and suggests a rewrite.

  • Only flagged posts are sent, never your whole queue
  • Claude or Gemini, with your own key (Gemini has a free tier)
  • It suggests; you decide what gets posted

Fair questions

Will it change my posts?

Only when you tap: rewrite, push back a day, or pull to drafts. It never publishes or deletes.

Where does the news come from?

Free public sources: Google News, the National Weather Service, Wikipedia, Google Trends, USGS, GDACS, FDA and CPSC recalls, plus a sensitive-days calendar.

Is my data sent to AI?

Only when you tap “Ask AI,” and only that post plus today's headlines. Your AI key stays in your browser.

Will it ever be wrong?

Sometimes, in both directions. It's a work in progress and a second set of eyes, not the last set: a human should still give every post a final read. Every flag shows its reason, and "Not relevant" teaches it what to ignore.

Isn't this a little dark?

We joke about brands that posted at the worst moment, never about the moment itself. The news it reads is real and happened to real people, and the app treats it that way.

What does it cost?

Nothing while it's in beta. The AI second opinion is optional and uses your own key (Gemini has a free tier).
