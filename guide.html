      How verdicts work

      Too soon

      A named person or place is in tragic news, your wording echoes a tragedy, a promo lands on a day of remembrance, or the link or date is broken.

      Give it a look

      Probably fine, but a human should glance: a weather alert where you are, a developing story, a tribute post on a solemn day.

      You’re good

      Nothing in today’s news, the calendar or the post itself raises a flag. Post away.
